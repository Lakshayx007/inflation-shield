What If?

What if you could see it coming — 6 months in advance?

We built a model that scores each company every quarter on a scale of 0 to 100. We call it the Margin Squeeze Score.

Do You Know?

We look at 6 vital signs of a company's pricing power

Think of these like a doctor's checkup — but for a company's ability to survive inflation.

💰
CPR
Cost Pass-Through
Can the company pass cost increases to its customers? A CPR above 1.0 means "yes — they raise prices even faster than costs rise." Below 1.0? They're absorbing the hit.
🎢
ICSI
Input Cost Volatility
How wildly are raw material costs swinging? A steel company facing 30% quarterly swings in iron ore prices is far more exposed than an IT company buying cloud credits.
📦
IBD
Inventory Buffer
Does the company stockpile raw materials as a cushion? More inventory days = more time before price increases hit the P&L. But it also ties up cash.
⏳
WCSS
Working Capital Stress
Are customers paying slower than suppliers demand payment? When debtor days exceed creditor days, cash flow gets squeezed — and margins follow.
🔒
OL
Fixed Cost Burden
How much of the company's cost structure is fixed (employees, rent, depreciation)? High fixed costs mean you can't easily cut expenses when revenue dips.
📊
SIB
Sector Sensitivity
Is this company more or less inflation-sensitive than its industry peers? A SIB above 1.0 means the company's margins move more than the sector average when inflation hits.

🚧 What we can't do (yet)

📅
Data ends in 2014
The model hasn't seen GST (2017), COVID-19 (2020), or post-pandemic commodity spikes. Retraining on fresh data is the natural next step.
🏢
Only 30 firms
Enough for gradient boosting, but too few for deep learning to really shine. Expanding to BSE 500 would unlock more statistical power.
🌍
No macro features
We don't include Brent crude prices, WPI inflation, or INR-USD exchange rates. Adding these would likely help commodity-sensitive firms.
📉
Sparse balance sheet data
Inventory and working capital data is ~85-92% missing before 2010. Two of our six features (IBD, WCSS) are weakened by this.
